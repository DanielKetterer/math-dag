## Missing routes and prerequisites the critic proposed

- fraction.unit.part-whole: needs counting.objects. The denominator b is the count of equal parts; this may be implied by partitioning.equal-shares.area but is not stated, and it is the same skill e2 uses for the numerator.
- fraction.unit.number-line: route. Measure route: 1/b is the length that laid off b times from 0 reaches 1, found by iterating a trial length and adjusting; the node's own 'equivalently' characterization, with no part-whole concept.
- fraction.non-unit.number-line: needs counting.objects. Step 2 counts a copies as they are laid off; probably carried by number-line.whole-number-lengths through the unit-fraction node, so low priority.
- fraction.as-number: needs new:number-line.order-by-position. The assessment asks 'more than 0? less than 1?', which needs 'farther right is greater' on the line. It may sit inside number-line.whole-number-lengths, but it should be stated.
- fraction.whole-number.as-fraction: route. Part-whole route (different facet): b/b is all b parts of size 1/b, which is the whole, so 1 = b/b; n/1 is n wholes. This is the usual 3.NF.3c-adjacent classroom argument and needs no number line.
- fraction.equivalent.simple.generate: route. Joining route: group equal numbers of parts (e.g. 4/6 in pairs) to find a simpler equivalent. The statement allows 'or join', which needs grouping, not splitting.
- fraction.compare.same-denominator: route. Part-whole route: equal-size parts in a bar or area model, so the fraction with more shaded parts is greater (the node's facet is measure, so this is a different meaning, but same level and type).
- fraction.compare.same-denominator: needs new:number-line.order-by-position. The number-line route (e28) needs 'farther right is greater' to conclude from more copies of 1/b.
- fraction.compare.same-numerator: route. Number-line route: a copies of 1/b versus a copies of 1/b', with the longer unit reaching farther right; needs unit-fraction comparison on the line.
- fraction.compare.record-symbols: route. Record a comparison of amounts of the same whole without the number concept: a same-denominator or model-based comparison result, written with >, =, <.
- fraction.equivalent.multiply-principle.generate: route. Compute the products n x a and n x b by skip counting or repeated addition rather than recalled facts.
- fraction.equivalent.multiply-principle.recognize: route. Recognize equivalence by locating both fractions on the number line and checking whether they coincide.
- fraction.equivalent.multiply-principle.recognize: needs new:fraction.equivalent.common-denominator-or-cross-check. As stated the procedure is unsound for non-equivalence: 4/6 and 6/9 are equivalent but neither denominator is a whole-number multiple of the other, and failing step 1 does not show 6/8 and 3/5 differ. A complete decision needs a common-denominator or position/size check.
- fraction.equivalent.multiply-principle.explain: route. Number-line justification: subdivide each unit interval into n equal pieces, so the point named by a/b is also named by (n x a)/(n x b); same level, measure facet.
- fraction.equivalent.multiply-principle.explain: needs new:reasoning.generality-of-argument. The assessment asks why the argument works for any n. That needs recognizing that the argument does not depend on the particular n, which no listed node teaches.
